Parloa — Enterprise Voice AI

A research case study examining how Voice AI can support customer service, and how to distinguish operational claims from evidence of customer value.

Read full case study on Notion ↗

Research question

How should a product team evaluate an enterprise Voice AI experience beyond the number of calls handled or the accuracy of intent recognition?

Context

Voice AI can support tasks such as answering common questions, identifying customer intent, routing a conversation, helping with appointment workflows and escalating to a human when needed. These capabilities are only valuable when they work reliably in the customer’s real context.

Areas reviewed

  • Response quality: Does the system understand the request and provide a relevant response?
  • Task completion: Can the customer complete the intended task, not merely finish a conversation?
  • Escalation: Can the system recognize when a person should take over?
  • Customer effort: Does the experience make the process easier, or does the customer need to repeat information?
  • Business impact: Are efficiency improvements balanced with service quality?

Key insight

Call volume and intent recognition are useful operational indicators, but they do not by themselves prove that a customer’s problem was resolved or that the customer was satisfied. A sound evaluation should combine operational measures with task outcomes, escalation quality and customer feedback.

Evidence principleSeparate vendor-reported outcomes from independently supported evidence, and make the remaining questions visible instead of treating every claim as proven.

What this demonstrates

This case study reflects research and analysis rather than direct ownership of Parloa’s product. It demonstrates a structured approach to AI product evaluation: identify the use case, define what success means for the user, inspect the evidence and document the gaps.
