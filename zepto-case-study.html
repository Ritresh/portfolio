Zepto — My Buckets

A proposed feature concept to make repeat shopping easier by letting users save frequently purchased items in reusable, customizable buckets.

Read full PRD on Notion ↗

The problem

People often buy the same household essentials repeatedly. Rebuilding a similar cart each time can be inconvenient, especially when shoppers are managing several recurring needs.

Proposed solution

My Buckets would let users create named collections—such as weekly essentials or breakfast items—then review and add those items to a cart when needed. AI-assisted suggestions could help users remember relevant items, while keeping the shopper in control.

Key product considerations

  • Users create, rename, edit and remove their own buckets.
  • Users can review the items before adding them to the cart.
  • Unavailable products should be clearly identified, with substitutions presented for user approval.
  • AI suggestions should be optional and explainable enough for users to understand why an item is suggested.

What the PRD covers

The document covers the user problem, proposed feature, user stories, scope and non-goals, risks, open questions, and potential pilot measures such as feature adoption, reorder behavior and acceptance of optional suggestions.

Status: proposed concept, not launched.This is a product proposal. It has not been presented as a shipped feature, and its baseline metrics or targets have not been validated.

Product lesson

Convenience features should reduce effort without taking control away from the user. A useful pilot would test whether buckets genuinely make repeat purchases easier before expanding the feature.
